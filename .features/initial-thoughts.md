The cookbook app should be for me and my family. This means it should be easy to use, intuitive, but also allow sharing.
It should provide a view on the recipes, ingredients, and meal plans, making it easy to browse, search, and manage them.

Rough ideas for the app's features and structure include:

- a recipe catalog should be the main focus, allowing users to browse, search, and view detailed recipe information.
- recipe creator mode, allowing users to add new recipes with ingredients, instructions, and tags and pictures
- a weekly meal planner that allows users to plan meals for each day
- add to shopping list functionality, allowing users to compile ingredients needed for planned meals
- the possibility to invite users to the shared cookbook app, allowing collaborative meal planning and recipe management
- cook mode, providing step-by-step instructions for preparing selected recipes including timers, the ingredient checklist for that step and the ability to move to the next step
